## 1. Executive Product Overview & The "Elevator Pitch"

### What is ForStore?
**ForStore** is a B2B SaaS in-store customer engagement and gamified loyalty platform purpose-built for **cafes, quick-service restaurants (QSRs), lounges, bars, and retail dining spaces**. 

It transforms **dead customer dwell time** (the 10–25 minutes patrons spend waiting for their food, coffee, or bill) into **high-margin revenue upsells, organic 5-star Google reviews, and verified first-party customer phone lists**.

### The 30-Second Elevator Pitch
> *"Every cafe owner loses money during table wait times. Customers stare at Instagram, complain about delays, and order the bare minimum. With ForStore, venues place custom-branded QR standees on each table. Customers scan and instantly play exciting 60-second games—**with zero app downloads or logins**. When they win, they unlock instant time-limited vouchers (e.g., 'Free Brownie with your Cappuccino' or '20% off next visit').

> Cafes see an immediate **₹35,000–₹60,000+ monthly revenue lift**, gather hundreds of repeat customer phone numbers, and never pay fixed software fees. During our launch, all plans are **₹0/month**—merchants only consume micro fuel credits when real patrons actually play."*

## 4. The 4-Step Customer Experience Loop

```
┌─────────────────┐       ┌─────────────────┐       ┌─────────────────┐       ┌─────────────────┐
│   STEP 01:      │       │   STEP 02:      │       │   STEP 03:      │       │   STEP 04:      │
│  Scan at Table  │ ────> │  Instant Play   │ ────> │ Unlock Reward   │ ────> │ Counter Claim   │
│ (Printable QR)  │       │(60s Mini-Game)  │       │(15-Min Voucher) │       │ (Staff PIN)     │
└─────────────────┘       └─────────────────┘       └─────────────────┘       └─────────────────┘
```

1. **Step 1: Scan at Table**
   - The customer sits down and notices a high-design table tent: *"Bored waiting? Scan to Play & Win Free Treats!"*
   - Camera scan immediately opens the cafe's white-labeled mobile arcade (`play.curaflowstudio.com` or custom cafe subdomain).
2. **Step 2: Instant Play (Zero App Download)**
   - Patron selects a game (e.g., *Barista Catch*, *Coffee Tower Stack*, or 2-player *Neon Air Hockey*).
   - Super-optimized HTML5/WebGL game runs at 60 FPS smoothly on iOS Safari and Android Chrome.
3. **Step 3: Unlock Reward & Dynamic Offer**
   - Scoring points unlocks a tier reward (e.g., Bronze: 10% off bill, Gold: Free Cookie/Dessert).
   - A digital voucher with a 15-minute countdown clock creates urgency to redeem immediately on the current bill.
4. **Step 4: Counter Claim via Staff PIN**
   - Patron shows their digital voucher code to the cashier/barista at the counter.
   - Staff taps `/claim/[code]`, inputs their secret 4-digit Staff PIN, and the voucher is marked redeemed with full audit logging.

## 7. Sales Discovery Call Playbook & Diagnostic Questions

When speaking with a cafe owner, manager, or food brand founder, follow this structured discovery flow:

### Phase 1: Diagnosing Dwell Time & Ticket Size
1. *"What is the typical wait time between a customer ordering and their main order hitting the table during weekend rush hours?"*
   - *Target Answer:* 12–25 minutes.
   - *Follow-up Hook:* *"During those 18 minutes, what are customers currently doing at their tables?"* (Almost always: staring at phones).
2. *"What is your current Average Order Value (AOV), and what percentage of customers order add-ons like desserts, mocktails, or appetizers?"*
   - *Target Answer:* Only 15–20% order add-ons.
   - *Follow-up Hook:* *"If we could give customers an interactive reason to add a ₹120 dessert or beverage while they're sitting there, what would that do to your daily register totals?"*

### Phase 2: Diagnosing Customer Retention & Remarketing
3. *"How do you currently capture customer phone numbers for weekend offers or event nights?"*
   - *Target Answer:* Bill punch POS (often skipped) or Zomato/Swiggy (who hide customer data).
   - *Follow-up Hook:* *"If you had a direct database of 500+ real customers who loved your cafe and visited in the last 30 days, would sending a WhatsApp blast fill your tables on a rainy Tuesday?"*

### Phase 3: Unveiling The Solution
4. *"Have you ever tried placing QR codes on tables, and why didn't you turn them into an arcade?"*
5. *"If you could roll this out tomorrow with zero tablets to buy, zero POS changes, and ₹0 monthly software fees, would you be open to running a 14-day test on 5 tables?"*

## 8. Objection Handling Playbook

### Objection 1: *"My customers just want to talk, they don't want to play games."*
- **Rebuttal:** *"That's exactly why we built 2-player tabletop games like Neon Air Hockey and Tap War! It's not people burying their heads in solitary apps—it's two friends or couples laughing and competing across the table on a single screen. Plus, customers who don't want to play simply don't scan; it never interrupts their conversation."*

### Objection 2: *"My waitstaff is already busy. They don't have time to manage game rewards."*
- **Rebuttal:** *"There is zero management for staff. The customer walks up to the counter, shows their 6-digit voucher pass, and your cashier enters a 4-digit PIN on their phone or browser. It takes under 3 seconds—faster than processing a debit card."*

### Objection 3: *"I don't want to give away free food; it hurts my food cost margins."*
- **Rebuttal:** *"You never give away high-cost mains! The Reward Studio is 100% under your control. You offer high-margin impulse items like 'Add Fries for ₹49 with any burger', or 'Buy 1 Coffee get 1 Brownie 50% Off'. These items cost you ₹15 to make, but generate an extra ₹150–₹250 on the customer's total bill."*

### Objection 4: *"What if a kid sits at the table and plays 50 times? Will my wallet drain?"*
- **Rebuttal:** *"We built the Anti-Abuse Fair-Play Shield specifically for this. The system hard-caps billing at 10 plays per customer per day. Any play from the 11th onward is 100% sponsored by our platform for free. You will never be overbilled by an arcade enthusiast."*

### Objection 5: *"We already have a QR code on our tables for the digital menu."*
- **Rebuttal:** *"That's the best part! You can embed your ForStore game arcade link right into your digital menu header, or place our compact 3-inch acrylic standee right next to the menu QR. Menu QRs are passive; game QRs are active and convert dwell time into upsells."*
